## Iteration 9 - 2026-08-19 20:37
**Story**: AR-009 - Schedule a job for every unresolved observation and never lose one to a rollback
**Status**: done
**Notes**: The experiment confirmed the rollback loss: a job scheduled inside a caller's transaction was discarded when the caller failed (drizzle transactions join the ambient effect/sql transaction as savepoints). scheduleUnresolvedResolutionJob now strips the ambient transaction service from its context so the job commits independently; regression test proves it survives. On the catalog filter, the review's assumption was wrong: a mapping row appears once an asset is observed, so a bare catalog entry means "never observed" and must not trigger research (the full-suite run proved this - the widened filter scheduled 12 jobs for unobserved catalog entries). Kept the two filters but made them named, documented constants (OBSERVED_UNRESOLVED_STATUSES vs CATALOG_REVIEWABLE_STATUSES) with tests for both rules. Also renamed the provider-neutral feeTransfers field to canonicalTransfers across contracts, repositories, providers, and tests (Coinbase's genuinely fee-specific leg-derivation contract keeps feeTransfers) and removed the String(transfer.amount) no-ops.
